Time-of-Use (TOU) Electricity Plans in Texas — Are They Right for You?

July 2026 · 5 min read
Home › Blog › Time-of-Use Plans

Time-of-Use (TOU) plans charge different prices at different times of day. If you can shift enough of your electricity use into cheaper hours, a TOU plan may reduce your monthly bill—but they aren't a universal win. This guide explains how TOU works in Texas and how to decide if it makes sense for your home.

How TOU pricing works

Instead of one flat cents-per-kWh price, TOU plans define two or more pricing periods (for example: on-peak, off-peak, super-off-peak). Each period has a different price. On most plans, on-peak hours are the most expensive and line up with late afternoon/early evening when demand is highest.

Who benefits from TOU plans?

  • Households that can shift usage: If you run heavy loads (washing, EV charging, pool pumps) during off-peak hours, you can save.
  • EV owners: Charging overnight typically matches cheap, off-peak windows.
  • Homes with smart automation: Smart plugs, thermostats, and timers make shifting easy.

Who might lose on TOU?

If most of your usage occurs during a wide on-peak window (evening cooking, AC use, electric heating), the higher on-peak price can outweigh off-peak savings. Also watch for narrow off-peak windows or high on-peak premiums—those plans are harder to win with.

How to evaluate a TOU offer

  1. Get your usage profile: Look at three months of usage on your utility or retailer account and note hourly or daily patterns if available.
  2. Compare using realistic hours: Use the plan's published TOU hours and apply your hourly usage to compute a sample bill (many retailers provide calculators).
  3. Read the EFL: Check the Electricity Facts Label for sample prices and the contract term. EFLs show the all-in average at common usage levels but not hourly splits—you need both pieces.

Practical tips to maximize savings

  • Program EV charging to run overnight or during the plan's off-peak window.
  • Run dishwashers and laundry late at night when feasible.
  • Use a smart thermostat to pre-cool or pre-heat your home ahead of on-peak hours.

Bottom line

TOU plans can save money for households willing and able to shift usage. They are especially powerful for EV owners and homes with automation.
