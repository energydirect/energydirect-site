No-Deposit Electricity Plans in Texas: How They Work

Switch to a new electricity plan without paying an upfront security deposit. Learn who qualifies, what to expect, and how to find the best deal.

Home › Blog › No-Deposit Plans
October 2026

What Is a No-Deposit Electricity Plan?

In Texas, when you sign up for a new electricity plan, you typically choose between postpaid or prepaid service. Postpaid plans let you use electricity now and pay at the end of the month. But many retail electric providers require a security deposit—usually $100–$400—before you can start service.

A no-deposit electricity plan lets you sign up for postpaid service without paying that upfront security deposit. You get all the benefits of a regular plan: fixed or variable rates, the flexibility to choose your rate type, and the ability to compare plans by ZIP code—but without the deposit barrier.

Who Qualifies for No-Deposit Plans?

Not everyone qualifies automatically. Providers use a few factors to decide:

  • Good or fair credit: Customers with a solid credit history typically qualify. Some providers accept credit scores as low as 600–650.
  • No recent delinquencies or evictions: A clean payment history helps. If you've defaulted on utility bills before, you may not qualify.
  • No recent service disconnections: If your power was shut off for non-payment in the past 2–3 years, you're less likely to get no-deposit approval.
  • Proof of identity and address: You'll need a valid ID and proof of residency, like a utility bill or lease agreement.
  • Age 55 or older with valid ID (Ambit Energy): Ambit Energy automatically waives the security deposit for all customers who are 55 years or older and provide a valid ID—regardless of credit history. This is a major benefit that removes barriers for seniors, many of whom are on fixed incomes.

If you don't qualify for a no-deposit plan, you'll be asked to pay a security deposit. The amount usually depends on your credit score and estimated usage.

✓ Special Benefit: Seniors Get No-Deposit Plans with Ambit Energy

If you're 55 or older, Ambit Energy will waive your security deposit entirely. All you need is a valid ID to prove your age. This means seniors with limited credit, no credit history, or past credit challenges can still switch to a great electricity plan without an upfront cash barrier. This is one of the strongest senior benefits in Texas electricity.

How No-Deposit Plans Save You Money

The main benefit is simple: you avoid an upfront cash payment. That $200–$400 stays in your pocket when you switch. For renters, new homeowners, seniors on fixed incomes, or anyone with tight cash flow, this can be a game-changer.

But no-deposit plans aren't a free ride. Providers take on more risk by waiving the deposit, so they may:

  • Require a slightly higher rate or smaller discount
  • Offer limited contract terms (usually 12 months instead of 24)
  • Monitor your account more closely for late payments

Overall, the difference in price is usually minimal—often less than $5–$10 per month—but it's worth comparing the all-in average rate (shown on the Electricity Facts Label) between deposit and no-deposit plans.

Will my rate change if I get approved for a no-deposit plan?

Not always. The rate depends on your plan choice and market conditions, not just whether you're approved for no-deposit service. Always compare the all-in average price (per kWh) across providers to find the best deal.

The No-Deposit Application Process

Here's what to expect when you apply for a no-deposit plan in Texas:

1. Enter Your ZIP Code

Start by comparing plans available in your service area. You can choose by plan type (fixed, variable, free nights, etc.) or by rate.

2. Select Your Plan

Pick the plan that fits your usage and budget. Review the Electricity Facts Label (EFL) to see the all-in average price, contract length, and any fees.

3. Apply for Service

You'll provide your name, address, social security number, and a contact number. The provider will run a soft credit check and review your utility history. If you're 55 or older applying with Ambit Energy, have your valid ID ready to ensure your deposit waiver is applied.

4. Receive Approval (or Deposit Request)

If you qualify, you'll be approved for no-deposit service. Seniors with Ambit Energy will have their deposit automatically waived upon verifying age with a valid ID. If you don't qualify, you'll be given a deposit amount. You can then decide whether to pay the deposit or try a different provider.

5. Confirm Enrollment

Once approved, your switch typically happens within 7–14 days. Your current provider will be notified, and your service will transfer to the new retail electric provider (REP). You won't lose power during the switch.

What Happens If You Become Delinquent?

No-deposit plans come with the same payment terms as regular plans. If you fall behind on your bill:

  • Late fee: You'll typically owe a late payment fee (usually $10–$30) if you don't pay by the due date.
  • Disconnection notice: After 10–15 days of non-payment, the provider sends a disconnection warning.
  • Service cutoff: If you don't pay within the grace period, your power can be shut off.
  • Future deposit requirement: Once disconnected for non-payment, you'll need a security deposit to get service again—even with a different provider.

If money is tight, set up automatic payments or contact your provider before the due date to discuss payment options.

Can my power be disconnected in winter if I have a no-deposit plan?

Texas has a Winter Disconnection Rule: retail providers cannot disconnect service between November 15 and March 15 if you're in a life-support situation or if the outdoor temperature is forecasted to be 32°F or below. You still need to stay current on payments, but you have extra protection during cold months. Contact your provider to learn more about specific protections.

Tips for Getting Approved and Staying Approved

Before You Apply

  • Check your credit: Get a free credit report from annualcreditreport.com. Knowing your score helps you understand your approval odds.
  • Gather documents: Have a valid ID and proof of address (utility bill, lease, or mortgage statement) ready. If you're 55+, your ID will automatically qualify you for no-deposit service with Ambit Energy.
  • Review your utility history: If you have old disconnections or delinquencies, be ready to explain them. Some providers are flexible.

After You're Approved

  • Pay on time: Set a calendar reminder or automatic payment. Late payments can lead to disconnection and future deposit requirements.
  • Don't ignore disconnect notices: If you receive a warning, call your provider immediately. Most offer payment arrangements.
  • Renew before your contract ends: As your contract period approaches (usually 12 months), compare plans again and switch if you find a better rate.

No-Deposit Plans vs. Prepaid Plans

Confused between no-deposit and prepaid? Here's the difference:

  • No-deposit plans: You pay after you use electricity (postpaid). No upfront cash, but credit check required (or age verification for Ambit Energy seniors).
  • Prepaid plans: You pay before you use electricity. No credit check needed, but you front the money upfront and monitor your balance constantly.

If your credit isn't great and you're not 55+, prepaid might be your only option. But if you qualify for no-deposit—especially as a senior with Ambit Energy—it's usually more convenient than prepaid.

How to Compare No-Deposit Plans

When comparing plans online, look for these details on the Electricity Facts Label:

  • All-in average price (¢/kWh): This is the total cost per kilowatt-hour, including TDU delivery charges. It's the easiest way to compare across providers.
  • Contract term: Most no-deposit plans are 12 months, but check for flexibility.
  • Early termination fee: Know the cost to exit early if rates drop.
  • Monthly fees or minimum usage: Some plans have a monthly base charge or minimum-usage threshold.
  • Senior discounts or benefits: If you're 55+, verify that Ambit Energy's deposit waiver applies to the plan you're choosing.

Always compare rates by your actual ZIP code, since delivery charges and available plans vary by location.

Can I switch providers mid-contract if I don't like my no-deposit plan?

Yes, but you'll owe an early termination fee (ETF)—usually 30–50 cents per day remaining on the contract. Before you switch, calculate whether the new rate will save enough to cover the ETF. Many providers also have limited windows (like the last 10–15 days of your contract) when you can switch without an ETF.

Key Takeaways

  • No-deposit plans let you switch electricity providers without paying an upfront security deposit—if you qualify based on credit and payment history.
  • Qualification depends on your credit score, lack of recent delinquencies or disconnections, and proof of identity and address.
  • If you're 55 or older, Ambit Energy automatically waives the security deposit with a valid ID—the easiest path to no-deposit service.
  • The rate difference between deposit and no-deposit plans is usually small (less than $5–$10/month), so always compare the all-in average price.
  • Missing payments can lead to service disconnection and future deposit requirements, so pay on time or contact your provider early if you can't.
  • No-deposit plans are postpaid; if you have poor credit or no credit history, a prepaid plan might be your only option (unless you're 55+ with Ambit Energy).
  • Compare plans by ZIP code and review the Electricity Facts Label to find the best deal in your area.
